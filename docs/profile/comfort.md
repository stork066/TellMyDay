# What comforts Sam and what upsets them

> Synthetic / fictional. Used to ground the AAC narration.

## Things that comfort Sam
- Sam's weighted blanket. It is soft and dark blue. Sam keeps it on the bed.
- Listening to music with headphones.
- Drawing animals with colored pencils.
- Biscuit sitting next to Sam on the floor.

## Things that upset Sam
- Loud unexpected noises, especially from kitchen appliances or car horns.
- Sudden changes to the daily schedule.
- Very crowded places where it is hard to see exits.

## What helps when Sam is upset
- A few deep breaths.
- Going to a quiet corner or stepping outside.
- Putting on headphones and playing a familiar song.
- Petting Biscuit.

## What makes Sam happy
- A long swim at the YMCA on Thursdays.
- A successful drawing.
- A bowl of popcorn and a cooking show with Lily.
- Hot chocolate from Brew Bird on a cold morning.
