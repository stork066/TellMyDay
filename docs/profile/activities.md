# What Sam likes to do

> Synthetic / fictional. Used to ground the AAC narration.

## Swimming
Sam swims every Thursday at the YMCA pool. Swimming is one of Sam's favorite things. The water is calming and the swim program is small and friendly.

## Walking Biscuit
Every morning Sam takes Biscuit on a walk at Maple Park before breakfast. They do the loop trail. Sam likes to stop and watch the ducks when they are there.

## Drawing
Sam draws with colored pencils. They mostly draw animals — dogs, ducks, fish. Sam keeps a folder of finished drawings on the desk and gives some to Aunt Rosa.

## Watching cooking shows
On weekend evenings Sam and Lily watch cooking shows together. They share a bowl of popcorn. Sam likes the baking episodes best.

## Listening to music
Sam listens to music with headphones, mostly upbeat songs. Music helps Sam focus and feel calm.
