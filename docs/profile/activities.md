# What Joey likes to do

> Synthetic / fictional. Used to ground the AAC narration.

## Swimming
Joey swims every Thursday at the YMCA pool. Swimming is one of Joey's favorite things. The water is calming and the swim program is small and friendly.

## Walking Biscuit
Every morning Joey takes Biscuit on a walk at Maple Park before breakfast. They do the loop trail. Joey likes to stop and watch the ducks when they are there.

## Drawing
Joey draws with colored pencils. They mostly draw animals — dogs, ducks, fish. Joey keeps a folder of finished drawings on the desk and gives some to Aunt Rosa.

## Watching cooking shows
On weekend evenings Joey and Lily watch cooking shows together. They share a bowl of popcorn. Joey likes the baking episodes best.

## Listening to music
Joey listens to music with headphones, mostly upbeat songs. Music helps Joey focus and feel calm.
