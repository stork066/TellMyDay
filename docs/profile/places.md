# Places Sam goes

> Synthetic / fictional. Used to ground the AAC narration.

## The YMCA pool
Sam goes to the YMCA pool every Thursday afternoon. The swim program runs from 4:00 to 5:00. Sam loves the warm water and the way the pool feels quiet under the surface. Marcus is in the same program.

## Maple Park
Maple Park is the park near Sam's house. Sam walks Biscuit there every morning. There is a loop trail with big maple trees and a small pond where ducks visit in the summer.

## Brew Bird coffee shop
Brew Bird is the coffee shop on the corner. Sam goes there on Saturday mornings with Mom and gets a hot chocolate with extra whipped cream. The staff know Sam's order.

## Aunt Rosa's house
Aunt Rosa lives across town in a small house with a yellow door. Sam goes there for Sunday dinner most weeks. There is always music playing in the kitchen.

## Home
Sam's bedroom is upstairs. It has a weighted blanket on the bed, drawing supplies on the desk, and a window that looks out at the backyard.
